User guide

How to use EquipTrack

Simple steps for borrowing and managing school equipment.

1

Find equipment

Open Equipment. Search by item name or asset code, or filter by category. Each card shows stock, condition and storage location.

2

Request a loan

Select Request loan, enter a quantity, choose a due date within 30 days and explain the school-related purpose.

3

Track approval

Open My loans. Pending requests can be cancelled. Approved requests show the due date and any coordinator note.

4

Return equipment

When the item is physically returned, select Mark returned. Stock is restored and the transaction stays in your history.

Coordinator guide

Review requests

Open All loans. Check the purpose, quantity, due date and current stock, then approve or deny with an optional note.

Manage inventory

Add or edit equipment from the catalogue. Quantities cannot be reduced below the number currently checked out.

Monitor overdue items

The dashboard highlights approved loans whose due date has passed. Open All loans to identify the borrower.

Review activity

The Audit log records authentication, equipment changes, decisions and returns for accountability.

Security, safety and privacy

  • Registration creates borrower accounts only; coordinator permissions cannot be self-selected.
  • Passwords are salted and hashed, must meet strength rules, and should never be shared.
  • Sign out on shared computers. Session cookies are HTTP-only and protected forms use CSRF tokens.
  • Only the minimum account and loan details are stored; SQL inputs are parameterised and server routes enforce roles.
  • For real deployment, the school should use HTTPS, approved identity login, backups and a retention policy.
  • Report damage, missing accessories or safety concerns to the coordinator before return.
